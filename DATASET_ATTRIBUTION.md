## Modification notice

This secondary-analysis project does not modify the above source files in place. It creates new, derived files by validating and joining tables, converting measurement units, constructing temporal and app-category features, generating participant-day and participant-level summaries, fitting models, and reporting aggregate results. See `DATA_CHANGES.md`.
