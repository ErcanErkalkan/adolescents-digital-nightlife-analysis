# Prediction Timepoint Policy

The current out-of-fold machine-learning task is **retrospective post-night reconstruction**, not an operational pre-bed forecast. The released time windows use bedtime and wake-time information obtained with the morning survey. Therefore same-night pre-bed/post-bed windows, sleep-window duration, and cyclical bedtime variables are not available before the target night is complete.

Permitted manuscript language: retrospective reconstruction, out-of-sample reconstruction, morning-informed reconstruction.

Prohibited manuscript language: real-time prediction, pre-bed forecast, early-warning model, deployable risk prediction.
